Ask us anything
How long does a full home interior take?

It depends on the size of the home and the scope of work. After the site visit we share a week-by-week timeline for design and execution, and send regular site updates on WhatsApp so you always know where things stand.

Do you work on single rooms?

Yes — from a single kitchen or bedroom to a complete turnkey home or office.

Will I see the design before work starts?

Always. You review layouts, materials and 3D visuals and sign off before anything is built.

How is pricing worked out?

You get an itemised quote based on your chosen materials, finishes and scope — every cabinet, light and fitting listed — so there are no surprises later.

Which areas of Bengaluru do you cover?

We work across Bengaluru — Indiranagar, Koramangala, Whitefield, HSR Layout, Jayanagar, Hebbal, Sarjapur Road and surrounding areas. Projects outside the city are taken on a case-by-case basis.
